Loop 2 layer 1 manual pass per voice-flow-learning-loop#5. Source: `recall_search` against `repo-recall-staging` across 28 broad-shape queries plus a walk of `~/.repo-recall/dispatch/` (empty on the Mac at run time, no signal there).

Frequency notes are recall-rank-shaped, not exact counts. `recall_search` returns top hits per query, so "many" means a phrase showed up across multiple unrelated queries with multiple distinct ref ids; "several" means three to five distinct ref ids on one query; "unclear" means it appeared but the count is ambiguous.

Trigger phrases are picked to avoid known mangle-prone tool names (coily, mcporter, ssm, gh, repo-recall) and to be acoustically distinct from natural prose. Two-syllable nouns or short verb-noun pairs that don't collide with English filler.
